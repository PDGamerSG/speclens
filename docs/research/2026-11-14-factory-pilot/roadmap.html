Evidence before wider use

The factory sets numeric limits by defect severity. This research does not establish production accuracy.

  1. Visible, identifiable parts

    Relevant defects survive real lighting and preprocessing. Physical part, lot, capture and revision are known.

  2. Controlled decisions and standards

    Every pilot result has human confirmation. Block retired rules before new model calls; quarantine calls affected by a withdrawal in progress. Evidence survives retry and job cleanup.

  3. Untouched evaluation

    Freeze baseline and approved memory. Keep physical parts, source derivatives, lots and sessions together; test on unseen parts.

  4. Separate errors from workload

    Report defective parts accepted, good parts rejected, Review coverage, failures, human effort and end-to-end delay separately. Preserve first attempts and retakes.

  5. Shadow operation and recovery

    Compare beside the current process, audit AI Accepts, exercise failures and rollback, and retain the factory's release procedure.

The value hypothesis can fail

Correction reuse already exists elsewhere. If held-out parts show no benefit, unacceptable regressions or excess review effort, narrow or revise the SpecLens proposition.
